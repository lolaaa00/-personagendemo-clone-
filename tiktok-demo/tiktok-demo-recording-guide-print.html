Recording Steps

1. Channels Overview ~10 seconds
  • Start on the Channels page (default view)
  • Show the dashboard with existing connected accounts
  • Click the TikTok card to begin the connection flow
2. OAuth Authorization ~15 seconds
  • Authorization modal appears — point out the three scopes requested
  • Scopes: video.upload, video.publish, user.info.basic
  • Click "Authorize PersonaGen" → wait for success toast
3. Create a Post ~25 seconds
  • Click Create Post in sidebar — connected profile bar is visible
  • Upload video: Click the upload zone (auto-simulates)
  • Edit caption: Modify the pre-filled text
  • Add hashtag: Type a tag and press Enter
  • Change privacy: Select a different option from the dropdown
  • Toggle disclosure: Turn on "Branded Content"
4. Publish with Confirmation ~15 seconds
  • Click "Publish to TikTok"
  • Confirmation modal shows caption, hashtags, and privacy setting
  • Click "Confirm & Publish" → wait for success toast
5. Disconnect Account ~10 seconds
  • Click Settings in sidebar
  • Show the disconnect option and token revocation explanation
  • Click "Disconnect TikTok" → confirm in modal
