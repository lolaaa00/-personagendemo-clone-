§1 Scope of Services

Provider agrees to deliver managed AI influencer generation services ("PersonaGen Platform") to Client under the terms described herein. The service encompasses the full lifecycle of autonomous AI-generated content creation, from persona generation to autonomous social media distribution.

1.1 Core Deliverables

  • AI Persona Generation — Creation of synthetic influencer identities including name, biography, visual appearance, and voice profile using proprietary AI models.
  • UGC Content Pipeline — Automated generation of user-generated-style content including images, captions, hashtags, and posting schedules tailored to Client's brand.
  • Account Automation — Programmatic creation and management of social media accounts for generated personas across agreed-upon platforms.
  • Analytics Dashboard — Real-time metrics dashboard tracking engagement, reach, conversions, and persona performance.
  • Managed Infrastructure — Dedicated server environment including container orchestration, DNS management, SSL certificates, CI/CD pipeline, and security hardening.
  • Ongoing Operations — Continuous monitoring, maintenance, optimization, and support of all deployed systems per the selected Plan tier.

1.2 Exclusions

Unless separately agreed in writing, the following are excluded from this Agreement:

  • Direct advertising spend or media buying on any platform
  • Legal compliance review of generated content (Client bears responsibility for content compliance in their jurisdiction)
  • Physical merchandise or product fulfillment
  • Third-party API costs exceeding the Provider-allocated allowance (e.g., excessive LLM usage beyond plan limits)

  3. Late Payment: Invoices unpaid for more than seven (7) days after the due date may result in service suspension until the balance is settled. Provider will give forty-eight (48) hours written notice via WhatsApp before any suspension.

§4 Term & Termination

  1. Initial Term: This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues on a month-to-month basis unless terminated as provided herein.
  2. Termination by Client: Client may terminate this Agreement at any time with thirty (30) days written notice via WhatsApp or email. Services will continue through the end of the paid billing period.
  3. Termination by Provider: Provider may terminate with thirty (30) days written notice if Client fails to cure a material breach within fifteen (15) days of notice.
  4. Effect of Termination: Upon termination:
    • Provider will deliver all Client data, credentials, source code, and generated assets within fourteen (14) business days.
    • Infrastructure (servers, domains, DNS) will be maintained for thirty (30) days post-termination to enable Client migration, after which it will be decommissioned.
    • Unused prepaid amounts for the current billing cycle are non-refundable, but no additional charges will be made.
  5. Prorated Refunds: If Client terminates within the first fourteen (14) days of an annual commitment (if applicable), a prorated refund of unused months will be issued minus a 15% early-termination administrative fee.

§10 Data Security & Infrastructure

  1. All Client infrastructure is provisioned on dedicated, isolated server instances (not shared hosting).
  2. All data in transit is encrypted via TLS 1.3. All domains are proxied through Cloudflare with DDoS protection enabled.
  3. Server access is restricted to Provider's authorized personnel via SSH key authentication. No password-based server access is permitted.
  4. Client credentials and API keys are stored in encrypted environment variables, never committed to source code repositories.
  5. Automated daily database backups are maintained with a 30-day retention window.
