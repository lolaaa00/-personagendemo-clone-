§1 Scope of Services

Provider agrees to deliver managed AI influencer generation services ("PersonaGen Platform") to Client under the terms described herein. The service encompasses the full lifecycle of autonomous AI-generated content creation, from persona generation to autonomous social media distribution. All infrastructure, accounts, and deployments are provisioned within Client's own accounts and credentials. Provider builds, deploys, and manages the platform on Client's behalf, maintaining collaborative access solely to perform contracted services. AI model execution capabilities are integrated through designated third-party AI model aggregators and direct model providers (such as OpenAI, Anthropic/Claude, or similar platforms) suited to the specific workflows scoped for the Client.

1.1 Core Deliverables

  • AI Persona Generation — Creation of synthetic influencer identities including name, biography, visual appearance, and voice profile via the designated model aggregators and direct providers.
  • UGC Content Pipeline — Automated generation of user-generated-style content including images, captions, hashtags, and posting schedules tailored to Client's brand.
  • Account Automation — Programmatic creation and management of social media accounts for generated personas across agreed-upon platforms.
  • Analytics Dashboard — Real-time metrics dashboard tracking engagement, reach, conversions, and persona performance.
  • Managed Infrastructure — Dedicated server environment including container orchestration, DNS management, SSL certificates, CI/CD pipeline, and security hardening.
  • Ongoing Operations — Continuous monitoring, maintenance, optimization, and support of all deployed systems per the selected Plan tier.
Account Setup & Optional BYOK
Client is strictly and mandatorily required to establish and fund their own accounts with the designated third-party AI aggregators or direct model providers (such as OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic/Claude, etc.) or maintain active platform subscriptions required by the system. Client shall pay all token, usage, and subscription fees directly to these providers. Optionally, Client may choose to supply their own API keys ("BYOK Keys") for injection into the designated aggregator or direct provider settings. BYOK modifier eligibility and details are defined in §2 and Exhibit A.

1.2 Exclusions

Unless separately agreed in writing, the following are excluded from this Agreement:

  • Direct advertising spend or media buying on any platform
  • Legal compliance review of generated content (Client bears sole and exclusive responsibility for verifying content compliance in their jurisdiction with FTC, FDA, and advertising regulations, including all medical, dietary supplement, and structure/function claims)
  • Physical merchandise or product fulfillment
  • All third-party API, token, subscription, and model consumption fees (which must be paid directly by Client to designated aggregators and direct providers)
  • Provider bears no responsibility for third-party API costs, vendor-imposed rate limits, usage caps, subscription lapses, or service disruptions originating from Client-owned or funded aggregator or provider accounts

§3 Payment Terms

  1. Billing Cycle: Monthly, billed in advance on the same calendar date as the first payment.
  2. Payment Method: Stripe subscription (automatic recurring charge). Client's payment link will be provided upon execution of this Agreement.
  3. Late Payment: Invoices unpaid for more than seven (7) days after the due date may result in service suspension until the balance is settled. Provider will give forty-eight (48) hours written notice via WhatsApp before any suspension.
  4. Plan Changes: Client may upgrade or downgrade their plan tier with thirty (30) days written notice. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
  5. No Setup Fee: There is no one-time onboarding or setup fee. The monthly subscription covers all onboarding, infrastructure provisioning, and initial build.
  6. Suspension & Lock Release: In the event of service suspension due to non-payment under §3.3, Provider shall automatically halt the automated content scheduling and rendering pipelines, and any regional or industry exclusivity locks reserved for Client shall be immediately released.

§4 Term & Termination

  1. Initial Term: This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues on a month-to-month basis unless terminated as provided herein.
  2. Termination by Client: Client may terminate this Agreement at any time with thirty (30) days written notice via WhatsApp or email. Services will continue through the end of the paid billing period.
  3. Termination by Provider: Provider may terminate with thirty (30) days written notice if Client fails to cure a material breach within fifteen (15) days of notice.
  4. Effect of Termination: Upon termination:
    • Provider will deliver all Client data, credentials, source code, and generated assets within fourteen (14) business days.
    • Infrastructure (servers, domains, DNS) will be maintained for thirty (30) days post-termination to enable Client migration, after which it will be decommissioned.
    • Unused prepaid amounts for the current billing cycle are non-refundable, but no additional charges will be made.
  5. Prorated Refunds: If Client terminates within the first fourteen (14) days of an annual commitment (if applicable), a prorated refund of unused months will be issued minus a 15% early-termination administrative fee.

§10 Data Security & Infrastructure

Named Technology Stack: Client's platform is deployed on the following infrastructure, all registered under Client's own accounts: Hostinger VPS (compute), EasyPanel + Docker (container orchestration), OpenRouter (AI model gateway), Cloudflare (DNS, SSL, DDoS protection), and GitHub (source code management & CI/CD). Provider maintains collaborative access to these accounts solely to perform contracted services.
  1. All Client infrastructure is provisioned on isolated server instances under Client's own hosting account. Growth tier clients receive a dedicated, container-isolated instance separate from all other client workloads.
  2. All data in transit is encrypted via TLS 1.3. All domains are proxied through Cloudflare with DDoS protection enabled.
  3. Server access is restricted to Provider's authorized personnel via SSH key authentication. No password-based server access is permitted.
  4. Client credentials and API keys — including any BYOK Keys provided by Client — are stored in encrypted environment variables, never committed to source code repositories. BYOK Keys are stored identically to Provider-managed credentials and are isolated to Client's service instance.
  5. Automated daily database backups are maintained with a 30-day retention window.
