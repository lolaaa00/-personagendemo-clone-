Agreement Details

What the Service Agreement Covers

Every clause protects both sides. No surprises, no hidden terms — here's exactly what's in the contract.

📋

Scope of Work

AI influencer persona generation, UGC content pipeline, social account automation, content scheduling, analytics, and full infrastructure management.

🧠

Intellectual Property

You own every persona, avatar, script, and content asset we generate. We retain ownership of the underlying platform tooling and automation code.

🚫

Out of Scope

Landing pages, e-commerce stores, mobile apps, non-AI-influencer projects, physical fulfillment, and paid advertising management.

💰

Pricing & Costs

Flat managed plan fee plus operational costs (AI APIs, proxies, image gen, CAPTCHA) passed through with agreed monthly caps.

⏱️

SLA & Response Times

Telegram < 4 hours. Project turnaround per complexity tier (1–3 days, 1–2 weeks, or 2–4 weeks). Extensions require your written approval.

🔐

Confidentiality & NDA

Mutual non-disclosure agreement. Your brand data, credentials, and business information are never shared with third parties.

🚪

Termination & Exit

30-day written notice by either party. Full data export (personas, content, configs) delivered within 14 days. Clean handoff guaranteed.

⚖️

Liability & Limits

Best-effort anti-detection for automated accounts. No guarantees on platform account survival rates. All data stored on your infrastructure.
